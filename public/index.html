The story. Across the country, people are out on the streets against the government. Tonight the call went out: everyone marches to Jantar Mantar, and whoever gets there first raises the Tiranga and claims the day for the people. The city has other plans.

Make it to Jantar Mantar. Police are coming out of 21 real stations across Delhi, they're putting up barricades on the roads into Jantar Mantar, and they'll take anyone they catch to the nearest police station. Duck into shops to shake them off, find a way around the barricades, and raise the Tiranga when you get there.
